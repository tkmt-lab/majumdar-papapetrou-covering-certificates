# Sources and access limits

Bibliographic and mathematical context is separate from the numerical
certificates. Checked through 26 October 2026; no exhaustive priority claim.

- Contopoulos, *Periodic orbits and chaos around two black holes*, Proc.
  R. Soc. Lond. A 431 (1990), 183–202,
  [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1098/rspa.1990.0126). Publisher metadata/abstract
  checked in the literature review; full publisher PDF unavailable.
- Dettmann, Frankel and Cornish, *Fractal basins and chaotic trajectories in
  multi-black-hole spacetimes*, Phys. Rev. D 50 (1994), R618–R621,
  [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.50.R618),
  [preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/9402027). Preprint and publisher
  metadata checked in the literature review.
- Cornish and Gibbons, *The tale of two centres*, CQG 14 (1997), 1865–1881,
  [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1088/0264-9381/14/7/020). Earlier symbolic context;
  entropy quantities with different coding/time conventions are not equated.
- Shipley and Dolan, *Binary black hole shadows, chaotic scattering and the
  Cantor set*, CQG 33 (2016), 175001,
  [text](https://arxiv.org/html/1603.04469v3). Model discussion in Section II.1
  checked during paper preparation.
- Bolotin and Kozlov, *Topological approach to the generalized n-center
  problem*, [text](https://arxiv.org/html/1705.04671v1),
  [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1070/RM9779). Theorem 2.1 and boundary convention
  checked in the primary source. Appendix A is our application to the
  compact optical core, not an assertion that those authors studied our strips.
- Zgliczyński and Gidea, *Covering relations for multidimensional dynamical
  systems*, JDE 202 (2004), 32–58,
  [author PDF](https://ww2.ii.uj.edu.pl/~zgliczyn/papers/wts/cov.pdf),
  [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jde.2004.03.013). Theorem 9 supplies the
  closed-chain realization step. Introduction PDF page 2 explicitly lists
  Hénon, Rössler and Lorenz applications. Those earlier application papers
  were not all newly read for this revision.
- Kapela, Mrozek, Wilczak and Zgliczyński, *CAPD::DynSys*,
  [preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.07097). The library is an external
  dependency, fixed by commit and archive hash in REPRODUCE.md.
- Pantig, *Symbolic dynamics of multi-black-hole photon scattering*,
  Physica D 498 (2026), 135415,
  [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.physd.2026.135415),
  [publisher record](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0167278926003118).
  The publisher search record confirms volume 498 and article 135415.
  Direct publisher retrieval returned 403 during this revision. Only the
  accessible abstract and earlier literature-team metadata/excerpts were
  available. The manuscript attributes its minimal summary to the abstract;
  no full-text comparison is claimed.

The supplied raw inputs are project-generated rational geometry and numerical
records. There are no redistributed full papers, third-party library source
archives or external catalogue datasets. Historical source hashes identify
provenance; they do not make an unreviewed paper a checked mathematical premise.
